# Wiring and safety

- Feed the Peltier bank from a dedicated 12 V supply through a 25 A fuse.
- Keep the mains side in the earthed enclosure; the low-voltage side is
  isolated by the DIN supply.
- Bond the cold plate to the enclosure earth before first power-on.
- The thermocouple is type K; observe polarity on the MAX31855 header.
